Introduction

Welcome to Tic Tac Toe, developed by RJ App Studio. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our app on your Android device.

We built Tic Tac Toe as a fun, simple, and completely private experience. By using this app, you agree to the terms of this policy.

Our Promise
Tic Tac Toe does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you — ever.
2

Information Collection and Use

Tic Tac Toe does not collect any personally identifiable information. We have no interest in who you are — only that you enjoy the game. The following types of data are never requested or stored:

✕ Full name
✕ Email address
✕ Phone number
✕ Location / GPS
✕ Camera or microphone
✕ Contacts or photos
✕ Payment information
✕ Account credentials

The app may access basic Android system data (such as device type or OS version) only to ensure proper functionality. This is never used to identify or profile you.

3

Log Data

If the app crashes or encounters an error, your device's operating system may generate log data automatically. This can include your device's IP address, device model, OS version, the time of the error, and related technical details.

Any such log data is used exclusively for diagnosing and resolving technical issues, is never linked to your personal identity, and is not sold or shared with any third party beyond what is strictly necessary for app maintenance.

4

Cookies

Tic Tac Toe does not use cookies of any kind. We do not track your behavior, store session tokens, or use any similar tracking technologies within the app.

Third-Party Note
Third-party services integrated into the app (such as Google Play Services) may use their own identifiers or tracking technologies. Please refer to their individual privacy policies for more information.

Data Security

Since Tic Tac Toe does not collect or store personal data, there is no personal information on our servers that could be compromised. We believe the best data protection is not collecting data in the first place.

We follow best practices in app development to ensure our software behaves safely on your device. That said, no electronic system can be guaranteed 100% secure. We appreciate your understanding.

7

Children's Privacy

Tic Tac Toe is a family-friendly game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Because we collect no personal data at all, the app is inherently safe for younger players. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about your child's use of this app, please contact us and we will respond promptly.
