Overview & Scope
📄

This Privacy Policy explains how RJ App Studio ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with FlipZoo: Kids Memory Match ("the App"), available on Google Play under the package name in.rjappstudio.flipzoo.

FlipZoo is a memory card matching game designed for children aged 2–12 and their families. Because this app is directed at children, we have designed it with the highest possible level of privacy protection — collecting the absolute minimum data required to operate the app.

✅ Our core commitment: FlipZoo does not require children to create accounts, does not collect personal information from children, does not include social features, and never shares any user data with third parties for marketing purposes.

This policy applies to all versions of FlipZoo on Android and covers all users including children, parents, and guardians.

2
Information We Collect
📊

FlipZoo is designed to be played completely offline with no user accounts required. The information we collect is minimal and limited strictly to what is necessary for the app to function.

2.1 Data Stored Locally On Your Device (Never Transmitted)

The following is stored only on the device and is never sent to our servers:

  • Game progress — levels completed, stars earned, worlds unlocked
  • Zoo collection data — animals rescued and named by the player
  • Daily streak count and last play date
  • Selected age profile (Toddler / Explorer / Champion)
  • Theme preferences and card back selections
  • Coin balance and power-up inventory
  • Parent-configured settings (screen time limits, bedtime mode, ad preferences)
💡 All game data is saved locally using Android's standard SharedPreferences and local storage. It is not backed up to any cloud server operated by RJ App Studio.

2.2 Data Collected by Google AdMob (Third-Party SDK)

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob is configured in child-directed mode for all users of this app. In child-directed mode, AdMob:

  • Does NOT use the Android Advertising ID (AAID) from child users
  • Does NOT serve interest-based or personalized ads
  • Only serves contextual, non-personalized ads appropriate for children
  • Complies with COPPA and Google Play Families Ads Program requirements

AdMob may collect limited technical data such as device type and general location (country/region level only) solely for the purpose of serving a single non-personalized ad. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

2.3 Crash & Performance Data (Anonymous, Aggregated)

If the app crashes, anonymous technical data (device model, Android version, app version) may be collected via Android's built-in crash reporting to help us fix bugs. This data contains no personal information and cannot be linked to any individual user or child.

Information We Do NOT Collect
🚫

We believe the clearest privacy statement is one that tells you exactly what we do not do. FlipZoo never collects or processes any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information from children
  • Precise GPS or real-time location data
  • Photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Android Advertising ID (AAID) — disabled for this app per Google Play Families policy
  • Social media profiles or third-party account logins
  • In-app chat messages or user-generated content
  • Contacts, call logs, or any data from other apps
  • Biometric data of any kind
  • Health or fitness data
  • Financial data (all purchases handled exclusively by Google Play billing)
  • Browsing history or cross-app tracking data
  • Any data that could personally identify a child
✅ We have never sold, and will never sell, any user data to any third party. Ever.
4
Advertising & AdMob Policy
📢

Our Ads Commitment to Parents

  • Zero ads during active gameplay — ads only appear on menu/home screens, never during a level
  • Rewarded ads (watch to earn coins) are always player-initiated — never auto-played or forced
  • All ads are served through Google AdMob Families Self-Certified SDK only
  • Child-directed treatment flag (setTagForChildDirectedTreatment(true)) is enabled for all ad requests
  • No interest-based advertising, no behavioral profiling
  • No third-party ad networks beyond Google AdMob
  • Parents can disable all rewarded ads in the Parent Dashboard
  • A permanent "Remove All Ads" one-time purchase is available for parents who prefer a completely ad-free experience

AdMob SDK Compliance

Our AdMob integration targets Android API Level 33+ and does not declare the AD_ID permission, ensuring the Android Advertising ID (AAID) is set to a string of zeros for all users of this app, in full compliance with Google Play Families data practices.

⚠️ For Parents: While we use child-directed settings, Google AdMob may still collect minimal technical information to serve a single contextual ad. This is governed by Google's own privacy policies. You may review Google's data practices at policies.google.com.
5
Information for Parents & Guardians
👨‍👩‍👧

Parental Controls Built Into the App

All sensitive app settings are protected behind a Parent Gate — a simple math verification that young children cannot bypass. Inside the Parent Dashboard, parents can:

  • Set daily screen time limits (the app will display a "zoo nap time" screen when reached)
  • Enable Bedtime Mode to automatically calm the game experience after a set time
  • Turn off all rewarded ads entirely
  • View this week's play time and levels completed
  • Manage and review all in-app purchases
  • Enable purchase blocking to prevent accidental IAP by children

In-App Purchases

All purchases in FlipZoo are processed exclusively through Google Play billing. We never collect or store payment card information. All IAP screens are behind the Parent Gate. Parents should use Android's built-in purchase authentication settings (fingerprint / PIN required for purchases) for an additional layer of protection.

Your Rights as a Parent

You have the right at any time to:

  • Review what game data is stored on your child's device
  • Delete all local game data by uninstalling the app or using Android's "Clear Data" option in App Settings
  • Contact us to ask about any data practices
  • Opt your child out of any future analytics (email us at the address below)

COPPA Compliance (USA)
🇺🇸

FlipZoo fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), which protects the online privacy of children under the age of 13 in the United States.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • We do not require children to provide any personal information to use the app
  • We do not use persistent identifiers to track children across apps or websites
  • We do not share children's information with third parties for marketing
  • Push notifications, if enabled, require parent email opt-in only — never a child's contact information
  • We do not enable any behavioral advertising targeting children

If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete that information immediately. If you believe this has occurred, please contact us at officialrjappstudio@gmail.com immediately.

GDPR Compliance (European Union)
🇪🇺

For users in the European Union and European Economic Area, we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Legal Basis for Processing

The minimal technical data processed by AdMob (in child-directed / non-personalized mode) is based on legitimate interest in providing a free app experience. No consent-based personal data processing occurs for child users.

Your GDPR Rights

  • Right of Access: Request information about any data associated with your use of the app
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of any data — for local data, simply uninstall the app or use Android's "Clear Data" function
  • Right to Restriction: Contact us to restrict any processing
  • Right to Object: Object to any processing of your data
  • Right to Portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at officialrjappstudio@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

📍 For EU/EEA users, AdMob operates under Google's EU data processing terms and does not serve personalized ads to users in child-directed apps regardless of region.
8
Data Security
🔐

Because FlipZoo does not transmit personal data to our own servers, the security risk is inherently minimal. Game data lives only on your child's device under Android's standard app sandboxing, which isolates it from other apps.

  • All game data is stored in Android's private app sandbox — inaccessible to other apps
  • No external server communication for game data
  • In-app purchases processed entirely by Google Play's secure billing infrastructure
  • No passwords or sensitive credentials ever stored by this app
  • Parent Gate protects all sensitive settings with a verification step
