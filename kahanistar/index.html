Introduction

RJ App Studio ("we", "us", or "our") operates the KahaniStar: Kids Moral Stories Android application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

KahaniStar is designed for children aged 2–12 and their families. We take children's privacy extremely seriously. This policy complies with:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) — United States
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — European Union
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 & Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — India
Short version KahaniStar does not collect any personal information from children or parents. All story progress is stored only on your device and never uploaded anywhere. The only third-party data collection is from Google AdMob, which we use to display ads in the free version.
2

Information we collect directly

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from users of this App.

KahaniStar does not require any of the following:

  • Account registration, login, or email address
  • Name, phone number, or postal address
  • Precise or approximate GPS location
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Access to contacts, calendar, or photos
  • Any files from your device storage
Local storage only Your story progress, earned badges, selected language, and age group preference are saved locally on your device using Android's secure app storage (Capacitor Preferences). This data never leaves your device and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the App.

4

In-app purchases

KahaniStar offers optional in-app purchases (story packs and premium access) processed through Google Play Billing. All payment information is handled entirely by Google Play — we do not receive, store, or process any payment card information.

Purchase records can be found in your Google account's purchase history. For refund queries, please contact Google Play support directly.

5

Children's privacy (COPPA compliance)

KahaniStar is designed for children under 13. We take the following steps to protect children's privacy in compliance with COPPA:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children
  • Children cannot create accounts or user profiles in the App
  • There are no social features, chat functions, or user-generated content
  • Child usage data is not linked to any personally identifiable information
  • AdMob is configured with TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT = true which restricts data collection per COPPA requirements
  • No behavioural profiling of children is performed by us
Parent or guardian notice If you believe we have inadvertently collected any personal information from a child, please contact us immediately at officialrjappstudio@gmail.com. We will delete such information within 48 hours.
6

Data storage & security

All App data (story progress, earned badges, language preference, age group) is stored locally on your device using Android's secure application storage (Capacitor Preferences API).

  • No data is transmitted to RJ App Studio servers
  • No cloud backup of user data is performed by us
  • All locally stored data is permanently deleted when you uninstall the App
  • We follow industry-standard secure coding practices in our application
  • All third-party API communication (AdMob) uses HTTPS encryption in transit
7

Analytics

We do not use any analytics SDK or platform. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Mixpanel, or any similar service.

The only diagnostic data collected is through AdMob's built-in crash reporting, which is described in Section 3 above and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

10

Advertising choices

You can opt out of personalised advertising on your Android device:

Android 12 and above:

  1. Open device Settings
  2. Go to Privacy > Ads
  3. Tap Delete Advertising ID

Android 11 and below:

  1. Open device Settings
  2. Go to Google > Ads
  3. Enable Opt out of Ads Personalisation

You can also visit aboutads.info/choices to manage interest-based advertising preferences.
