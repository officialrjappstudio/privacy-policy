Privacy Policy

AgeWise: Birthday & Age Calculator

Effective Date: March 16, 2025  ·  Last Updated: November 15, 2026
Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how AgeWise: Birthday & Age Calculator ("the App"), developed by RJ App Studio, handles your information. Please read it carefully before using the App.

📋 1. Overview

AgeWise is a simple utility app that calculates your exact age, shows a countdown to your next birthday, and provides fun date-based facts. The calculators work on your device and do not need internet.

In short: Your names, birth dates, saved birthdays and settings stay on your device. The App shows one banner ad served by Google AdMob, and it sends usage events and crash reports to Google Firebase. These never include your names or birth dates.

📊 2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

RJ App Studio does not ask you to create an account or sign up, and we do not receive your names or birth dates. The App uses these Google services:

  • Google AdMob (ads): may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can show approximate location), device information and ad interactions
  • Firebase Analytics (usage events): for example, which tool was opened
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports): reports sent when the App crashes

Details are in section 5.

📱 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device On-Device Only

The names, birth dates, saved birthdays and settings you enter are stored only on your device using local device storage.

  • This data is never sent to RJ App Studio or anyone else
  • There is no account or sign-up
  • This data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in your device settings
Note: If your device has a system backup enabled (e.g. Google Backup), your device's own backup service may include this App's local data. This is governed by your device manufacturer's and Google's privacy policies, not ours.

🔐 4. App Permissions

AgeWise requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

  • Notifications (optional) – only if you turn on birthday reminders. Reminders are created on your device
  • Internet access – used for ads, analytics and crash reports. The calculators themselves work without internet

The App does not ask for camera, contacts or location permission.

🔗 5. Third-Party Services

Ads (Google AdMob). The App shows one banner ad served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can show approximate location), device information and ad interactions to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the App asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in the App. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase). The App sends usage events (for example, which tool was opened) and crash reports to Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics). We use them to fix bugs and improve the App. They never include your names or birth dates. Learn more: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

The App has no social media login.

👶 6. Children's Privacy

AgeWise is for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, please contact us and we will help.

🔒 7. Data Security

The data you enter stays on your device, so its security depends on the security of your device (e.g. screen lock, encryption).

We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and using device-level security features to protect your locally stored information.

🗂️ 8. Data Retention & Deletion

No account: AgeWise has no account to delete. The names and birth dates you enter exist only on your device, and you remove them on your device.

All data you enter in AgeWise is kept until you choose to delete it. You can remove it by:

  • Uninstalling the App from your device
  • Going to Settings → Apps → AgeWise → Clear Data on your Android device
  • Using the in-app delete option for individual saved profiles (if available)

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. If you have questions or wish to submit a data request, please contact us at officialrjappstudio@gmail.com and we will respond within 5 business days.

⚖️ 9. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights regarding personal data under laws such as the GDPR (EU) or CCPA (California). RJ App Studio does not receive your names or birth dates. Ad, analytics and crash data is handled by Google under the policies linked in section 5, and you can change your ad consent choice in the App.

If you have any questions or concerns about your privacy, you are welcome to contact us at any time using the details below.
