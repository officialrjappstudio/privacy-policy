01

Overview

RJ App Studio ("we", "our", "us") built SIP & EMI Calculator as a free utility app. This page explains what information is handled when you use the app and who handles it.

The app is free and supported by advertising through Google AdMob, and uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how the app is used. Both are provided by Google and may collect certain device and usage information. All financial values you enter, and every plan you save, are stored and processed on your device only; we never collect or receive them, and neither does any advertiser.

02

Information collected

There are two data flows in this app, both handled by Google as a service provider:

Data we receive directly — none about you. We run no servers or user database, and you do not create an account. We do not receive your name, contact details, financial inputs or saved plans. We do see anonymous, aggregated usage statistics in the Firebase console (see Section 04).

Data collected by Google AdMob (ads). As part of displaying advertisements, AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device type, model, and Android OS version
  • IP address (used to infer general location for ad targeting)
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks) and crash and diagnostic information
  • App language and locale settings

Data collected by Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics). When analytics is on, it may collect:

  • An app-instance identifier generated for this installation (it is not your Advertising ID, which analytics does not use here)
  • Device model, Android version, app version, language and approximate location from your IP address
  • Anonymous events such as which calculator was opened, whether a plan was saved or pinned, and how long since you first opened the app, grouped into ranges

Event data never includes any amount, rate, name or goal you typed.

Google Privacy Policy AdMob data practices

04

Analytics & your consent choice

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which features are used and whether the app works well, so we can improve it. It is used for analytics only, and the Advertising ID is not collected by it.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland: when you first open the app, Google's consent message asks whether you agree to the use of your device information for the purposes shown, including analytics and advertising. Analytics stays off unless you agree. You can choose "Do not consent" and still use every feature. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices; if you withdraw, analytics stops and the identifier stored for this installation is reset.

In other regions, analytics runs by default and the consent message is not shown. You can stop it at any time by uninstalling the app or resetting your device identifiers.

05

Your financial inputs and saved plans

Every value you enter — loan amounts, interest rates, investment figures, or any other number — is calculated on your device. If you choose Save plan, the plan (its name, goal, the figures you entered and any contributions, balances or prepayments you record) is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is:

  • Never sent to us (we operate no servers) and never accessible to Google AdMob, Firebase or any advertiser
  • Kept until you delete the plan or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes it
  • Not included in analytics: events record only that an action happened, never the amounts

Optional monthly reminders are scheduled on your phone and send nothing anywhere. The optional fingerprint lock uses Android's built-in biometric prompt; we never receive or store your fingerprint. Images and PDFs you share are created on your phone and handed to the Android share sheet.

07

Children's privacy

SIP & EMI Calculator is a general-purpose utility intended for users aged 13 and above. It is not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Google AdMob is configured to serve general-audience ads in this app, not interest-based ads targeted at children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 is using this app, please contact us and we will ensure appropriate measures are taken in line with COPPA and applicable Indian laws.

08

Your rights

You have the following controls:

  • Agree, decline or change your consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland) — Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app
  • Opt out of personalised ads — ads here are already non-personalised; you can also use Android Settings as described in Section 03
  • Reset your Advertising ID — breaks any previously built ad interest profile
  • Delete your saved plans — delete them in the app, or uninstall the app
  • Request Google data deletion — visit myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy for data held by Google
  • Contact us directly — we hold no personal data about you but will help with any question, access or deletion request
