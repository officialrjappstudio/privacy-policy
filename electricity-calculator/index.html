01

Overview & scope

RJ App Studio ("we", "our", "us") is an independent app developer based in India. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with the mobile applications we publish on Google Play Store, including but not limited to:

  • Financial Calculator (EMI, SIP, loan, and investment tools)
  • Electricity Bill Calculator (power consumption and tariff tools)
  • Any future apps published under the RJ App Studio developer account

By installing or using any of our apps, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app and discontinue use.

Our apps are utility tools designed to perform local calculations. We do not operate any servers, databases, or backend infrastructure that receives your data. The only third-party service integrated into our apps is Google AdMob for advertising, which is disclosed fully in Section 03.

03

Google AdMob & advertising

Our apps are free to use and are supported by advertising through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob enables us to show ads to users without charging for the app.

The following ad formats are used across our apps:

Format 1
Banner ads
Small rectangular ads displayed at the bottom of screens. Remain visible during use.
Format 2
Interstitial ads
Full-screen ads shown at natural transition points between screens. Always dismissible by the user.

To serve these ads, AdMob's SDK automatically collects certain technical data as listed in Section 02. This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. We have no access to this data — it goes directly to Google's servers.

Google's data practices

You can review how Google handles AdMob data at the links below:

Google Privacy Policy AdMob data practices Manage ad settings

To opt out of personalised ads on Android:

Android 12+: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID

Android 11 and below: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation

After opting out you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised based on your interests.

04

How your data is used

By RJ App Studio: We do not collect any data, therefore we do not use any data. All calculations performed in our apps (EMI, SIP, electricity bill, power consumption, solar estimates, etc.) are executed entirely on your device using local code. No result or input is ever sent to us.

By Google AdMob: The technical data collected by AdMob (advertising ID, device info, IP address) is used solely for:

  • Selecting and displaying relevant advertisements
  • Measuring ad performance (impressions and clicks)
  • Fraud prevention and ad quality enforcement
  • Complying with Google's own legal obligations

AdMob does not have access to any financial figures, electricity consumption data, or any other values you enter into our calculators. These remain entirely on your device.

05

Data sharing & disclosure

RJ App Studio does not share, sell, rent, or trade any personal information with third parties — because we collect none.

The only third-party data flow is from your device directly to Google AdMob's servers, which operates independently under Google's privacy policy. We are not a party to that data exchange and cannot access or control it beyond choosing to integrate AdMob into our apps.

We may disclose information in the following exceptional circumstances:

  • Legal compliance: If required by applicable Indian law (IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023), court order, or lawful government request — though as we hold no personal data, compliance in practice means confirming that we hold nothing
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of RJ App Studio, our users, or the public, to the extent required by law
  • Business transfer: In connection with any merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, where the new entity would be bound by this policy

07

Data retention & deletion

Data retained by RJ App Studio: None. We hold no personal data, so there is nothing to delete.

Data stored locally on your device: Some apps may store your preferences (such as selected state, default load settings) using the device's local storage (Android SharedPreferences or equivalent). This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by:

  • Going to Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Storage → Clear Data on your Android device
  • Uninstalling the app, which removes all locally stored preferences

Data held by Google AdMob: For requests to delete data held by Google, please visit myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. You can also reset your Google Advertising ID at any time from Android device settings, which effectively creates a fresh profile with no history.

08

Children's privacy (COPPA & DPDP)

Our apps are general-purpose utility tools not specifically directed at children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 18 where the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 applies additional protections for minors).

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children of any age. Because we collect no personal data from any user, children are subject to the same data treatment — which is none — as adult users.

With respect to advertising: our apps are configured in AdMob as general-audience apps, not directed at children. We do not use the "child-directed" treatment setting which would disable personalised ads, as our apps are general-audience financial and utility tools. Parents who prefer that their children not see personalised ads can delete the Advertising ID on the child's device (see Section 09).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 is using our apps and you have concerns about any data collection, please contact us at officialrjappstudio@gmail.com. We will investigate and take appropriate action promptly.

09

Your rights & opt-out controls

Under applicable laws including India's DPDP Act 2023 and general data protection principles, you have the following rights:

  • Right to know: This policy fully discloses all data practices. RJ App Studio holds no personal data about you.
  • Right to opt out of personalised ads: See the Android device steps in Section 03. You will continue to see ads but they will not be interest-based.
  • Right to reset your ad profile: Delete and regenerate your Advertising ID via Android Settings → Google → Ads. This breaks any previously built ad interest history.
  • Right to manage Google's data: Visit myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy to manage or delete data held by Google services including AdMob.
  • Right to contact us: For any privacy concern, email us at officialrjappstudio@gmail.com. As we hold no personal data, most requests will be answered with confirmation that no data is held.
  • Right to uninstall: You may uninstall any of our apps at any time. This removes all locally stored preferences immediately.

Security

Since RJ App Studio does not collect or store personal data, the primary security consideration is the safety of your locally stored preferences and the integrity of the app itself.

We take the following measures to protect users:

  • Our apps are distributed exclusively through the official Google Play Store, which provides code integrity verification
  • We do not store any financial data, calculation history, or sensitive inputs in persistent device storage
  • We do not transmit any user-entered data over any network
  • We follow secure coding practices and keep dependencies (including AdMob SDK) updated to their latest stable versions

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. However, because we hold no personal data, the risk to users from any breach of our systems is negligible — there is nothing of personal value to breach.
