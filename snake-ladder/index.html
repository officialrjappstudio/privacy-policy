1. Introduction

Welcome to Snake & Ladder, developed by RJ App Studio (com.rjappstudio.snakeladder). This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use it on your Android device.

Snake & Ladder is a classic offline board game. It is designed to work entirely on your device — no internet connection is required, no accounts are needed, and no personal data is ever collected, stored, or transmitted.

✅ Short version: We don't collect any data. Your game stays on your device.

📦

🔑

3. App Permissions

Snake & Ladder requests no Android permissions at runtime. The app does not request access to storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other sensitive device resource.

⚠️ If your device shows any system-level permission dialogs, they are generated by the Android OS itself (e.g., installing from unknown sources) — not by this app.

📡

4. Internet & Network Access

This application is fully offline. It does not connect to the internet, any external server, or any third-party service. No data is sent from your device to any remote endpoint — ever.

  • No API calls are made.
  • No cloud saves or sync.
  • No advertisements are fetched.
  • No social or analytics SDKs are integrated.
🔗

5. Third-Party Services

The app is built using Apache Capacitor as a native bridge. Capacitor is an open-source framework that does not collect or transmit any user data on its own. No third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries (such as Google AdMob, Firebase, Facebook SDK, etc.) are embedded in this application.

Distribution through the Google Play Store means that Google's own terms of service and privacy policy apply to the download and installation process. We have no control over data Google collects as a platform. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details.

👶

6. Children's Privacy

Snake & Ladder is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. Because the application collects absolutely no personal data, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent international regulations (e.g., GDPR-K, COPPA 2.0).

✅ No personal data is collected from any user, regardless of age.

Parents and guardians can allow children to use this app with confidence that no information about their child is collected, stored, or shared.

🛡️

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no user data at risk. All game state (player names, positions, scores) exists only in your device's temporary memory (RAM) and is discarded when you close the app.

The app binary is signed with a developer certificate to ensure integrity and authenticity when distributed through Google Play.

🗄️

8. Data Retention

No personal data is retained. Player names entered in the game setup screen are held in-memory only for the duration of that session and are permanently deleted when the app is closed or a new game is started. No data is written to persistent storage on your device.

⚖️

9. Your Rights (GDPR & Global)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and similar laws, users have rights including access, correction, deletion, and portability of their personal data.

Because Snake & Ladder collects no personal data, these rights are satisfied by default — there is no data to access, correct, delete, or port.

If you believe we may hold any information about you, please contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

🔔

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

We will notify users of any material changes through a notice within the application or via the Google Play Store listing.
