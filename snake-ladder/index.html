1. Introduction

Welcome to Snakes & Ladders: Saanp Seedi, developed by RJ App Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles when you use it on your Android device, why, and the choices you have.

The game itself works offline and needs no account. The app includes these Google services: Google AdMob, which shows ads: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example, “watch an ad to unlock a board for 24 hours”), and an occasional full-screen ad between games; Google Analytics for Firebase, which tells us how the game is used so we can improve it; and Firebase Crashlytics, which reports crashes and errors so we can fix them. These services collect limited device and usage information, described below.

✅ Short version: we never ask for your name, email or phone number. Your games, stats, streak and awards are saved only on your device. Google collects limited device and usage data to show ads, report crashes and produce anonymous usage statistics. The app sells nothing and takes no payments.

📦

2. Information Collected

RJ App Studio does not run its own servers and does not receive your personal information directly. The Google services in the app collect the following:

🔑

3. App Permissions

The app asks for one optional runtime permission: notifications, used only for the daily streak reminder. It is requested once you have a streak worth keeping, you can refuse it, and you can switch the reminder off at any time in Settings — the game is fully playable either way. The app also declares these standard permissions, which Android grants automatically: Internet and network state (to load ads), and Advertising ID (used by AdMob and Google Analytics). It does not access storage, camera, microphone, contacts or your precise location.

📡

4. Internet & Network Access

You can play without an internet connection. When your device is online, the app connects to Google’s servers to load ads and to send usage statistics and crash reports.

  • No cloud saves or sync: your games stay on your device.
  • Rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. A full-screen ad may also appear between games, never during one: at most once every three finished games, and not at all during your first few games.
  • No social network SDKs are integrated.
🔗

5. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob serves the optional rewarded ads and the occasional between-games ad. Ad content is limited to family-appropriate ratings. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with Privacy options on the game’s main screen. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google Analytics for Firebase gives us aggregated statistics such as how many games are played. We use it only to improve the game. See the Firebase privacy information.

Firebase Crashlytics sends us a report when the app crashes or hits an error, including the device model, operating system version and a stack trace. We use it only to find and fix bugs. See the Firebase privacy information.

The app is built with Capacitor, an open-source framework that does not collect data on its own. Google’s own Privacy Policy applies to data collected by Google services and by Google Play.

👶

6. Children's Privacy

Snakes & Ladders: Saanp Seedi is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads in the app are limited to content rated suitable for general and family audiences.

If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and want data associated with their device removed, contact us at the address below and we will help you request deletion from Google.

🛡️

7. Data Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. Saved games and player names stay in the app’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

The app is signed with a developer certificate to ensure integrity and authenticity when distributed through Google Play.

🗄️

8. Data Retention

Saved games, stats, streaks, awards and board unlocks stay on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Google keeps usage statistics according to its own retention settings (for Google Analytics, event-level data is kept for 2 months by default), and advertising data according to Google’s policies.

⚖️

9. Your Choices & Rights

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, change your ad consent with Privacy options on the main screen.
  • Switch off the daily streak reminder in the app’s Settings, or in Android’s notification settings.
  • Delete your saved games and stats by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Under GDPR, CCPA, India’s DPDP Act and similar laws you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Because RJ App Studio does not hold your personal data directly, we will help you exercise these rights with Google. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.
