Privacy Policy

Overview

Last updated: 28 November 2026.

RJ App Studio publishes the Income Tax Calculator app for Android as a free, ad-supported app. This page explains what information the app handles, what stays on your device, what is shared with third-party services, and the choices you have.

The short version: the income, deduction and tax figures you enter never leave your device. The app has no accounts and no servers of ours that receive your data. It shows ads served by Google AdMob, and it can receive tax-news notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Those Google services receive device-level information described below, never your tax figures.

Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following is saved in the app’s private storage on your phone and is never uploaded to us or to any third party:

  • The income, deduction and investment amounts you enter, and the tax results calculated from them;
  • Calculations you choose to save to History, and the name you give each one;
  • Your settings, such as theme, default financial year and reminder preference;
  • Counters the app uses to limit how often it shows ads or asks for a Play Store rating.

All tax calculations and the in-app “Tax Advisor” insights are computed on your device. Nothing you type is sent to a server for processing.

When you export a PDF, share an image, or print, the app hands the file to Android’s share or print screen and you choose where it goes. We do not receive a copy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, native, full-screen and optional rewarded video ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Your device’s advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system version and app version;
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostic data.

Ad requests from this app contain no keywords or content from your calculations.

Google’s use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. More about how Google uses data from apps that show its ads: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your Ad Choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad is requested, the app shows Google’s consent form where the law requires it. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices in the app.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Ad-free pass: you can choose to watch one rewarded video ad to switch off all other ads in the app for 24 hours. This is optional; nothing in the app requires it.

Notifications

Deadline reminders (advance tax, filing and similar dates) are scheduled on your device and need no network connection.

Tax-news updates are delivered through Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging when you turn on Tax reminders. Your device subscribes to a shared news topic; we do not collect or store a list of devices. Firebase processes a device instance identifier to deliver messages, under the Firebase privacy terms. You can turn this off at any time in the app’s Settings or in Android’s notification settings.

Ratings

The app may occasionally ask you to rate it using Google Play’s in-app review screen. That screen is run by Google Play; we only learn what you choose to post publicly on the store.

Data Security and Deletion

Information sent to Google’s services is encrypted in transit. Your tax data stays in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read.

You can delete your saved calculations and current draft at any time with Settings → Clear all data, or remove everything by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy of your data on any server, there is nothing further for us to delete. For data held by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

Children’s Privacy

The app is a tax tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Not Tax Advice

The app provides estimates based on published tax rules. It is not tax, legal or financial advice. For a precise assessment, please consult a qualified tax professional.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version is always on this page, with its date at the top. Changes take effect when they are posted.
