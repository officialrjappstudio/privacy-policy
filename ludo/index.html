Privacy Policy

Ludo · Last updated: October 27, 2026
In short: Ludo works without an account. If you play online or use the leaderboard, the game creates a random anonymous player ID and shares your display name, avatar and points. Signing in with Google is optional and only used to keep your progress on a new phone. We do not sell your data and the game has no ads.

This Privacy Policy explains what the Ludo mobile application (the “App,” the “Game,” “we,” “us”) collects, why, and the choices you have. We are RJ App Studio.

1. Information the Game collects

  • Anonymous player ID. When you first open the App, Firebase Authentication creates a random ID for your device. It is not your real identity. It lets the Game remember your points and rejoin an online room.
  • Display name and avatar. The nickname you type and the avatar you pick. They are shown to other players in online games and on the public leaderboard. Please do not use your real name.
  • Game progress. Your points (total and for the current week), level, coins, games played and won, and daily reward streak. Points and level are stored in our database (Cloud Firestore) so the leaderboard can rank players.
  • Online play. In a room or quick-play match, the multiplayer server receives your display name, player ID, level, the room code, and your game actions (dice rolls, pawn moves, emojis and quick chat phrases). Rooms are kept in the server’s temporary memory and discarded shortly after the match ends.
  • Optional Google sign-in. If you choose “Sign in with Google” on the Profile screen, Google gives the Game your Google account ID, display name and email address. Firebase Authentication stores them with your player ID so your progress can be restored on a new phone. The Game does not show your email address to other players.
  • Usage and device data (analytics). The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the Game is used (for example, that points were earned) and which app version and device type is used. This includes a Firebase app-instance identifier. The Game does not use or collect the advertising ID.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Your location, contacts, photos, files, phone number or payment details
  • The advertising ID. The App does not request that permission.
  • Free-text chat. Chat in the Game is limited to emojis and ready-made phrases.

3. How we use it

  • To run the Game: online rooms, quick play, levels and leaderboards.
  • To keep your progress and, if you sign in, restore it on a new phone (account management).
  • To understand how the Game is used and to fix problems (analytics).

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not build advertising profiles.

4. Who processes it

The Game uses these service providers, which process data for us under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics) and Google Sign-In.
  • A cloud hosting provider that runs our multiplayer server.

Data is encrypted in transit. Your display name, avatar and points are public to other players through the leaderboard.

5. Stored on your device

The Game also saves settings (sound, music and vibration), your display name, avatar, stats, coins and streaks in the App’s private storage on your device. Clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling removes this local data.

6. Retention and deleting your data

Leaderboard entries and account data are kept until you ask us to delete them. To delete your data (player ID, display name, avatar, points, and any linked Google account information) email officialrjappstudio@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Ludo data”. If you are signed in with Google, send the request from the same Google account email. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also uninstall the App to remove local data.

7. Device permissions

The App uses network access (online play, leaderboard, sign-in), vibration (haptic feedback) and the share sheet (only when you tap to invite a friend with a room code). We do not access your contacts.

8. Age

Ludo is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of the Google Play Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use Google’s security rules so a player can change only their own row, and encrypt data in transit. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
