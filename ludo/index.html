Privacy Policy

Ludo · Last updated: June 13, 2026
In short: Ludo is a board game you can play offline or online. We do not require an account, we do not ask for your email or phone number, and we do not sell your data. The little information the game uses (like your display name and game stats) stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Ludo mobile application (the “App,” the “Game,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use it. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Game does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect:

  • Your real name, email address, or phone number
  • Your contacts, photos, files, or location
  • Government IDs, payment card details, or financial information
  • Precise device identifiers used for advertising

2. Information Stored On Your Device

To make the Game work, a small amount of data is saved locally on your device (using your device’s local storage) and is not transmitted to us except as needed for online play:

  • Display name — the nickname you optionally type when starting a game.
  • Guest ID — a random, anonymous identifier generated on your device so you can rejoin an online room after a disconnect. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • Game preferences — such as your sound on/off setting.
  • Game progress & stats — local counts like games played, wins, captures, dice rolls, daily-challenge progress, and streaks.

You can clear all of this at any time by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

3. Online Multiplayer

If you choose Play Online, the Game connects to our game server to let you play with friends in real time. During an online match, the following is sent to the server purely to run the game:

  • Your chosen display name and anonymous guest ID
  • The room code you create or join
  • Game actions — dice rolls, token moves, and emoji reactions

This data is kept only in the server’s temporary memory for the duration of a game session and is automatically discarded shortly after the room ends (rooms expire automatically). We do not build profiles, and online game data is not stored long-term or used for any purpose other than running your match.

Online play is optional. If you only play offline (Pass & Play or vs AI), no game data leaves your device.

4. Device Permissions

The App may use the following device capabilities:

Network access Vibration / Haptics Share sheet

  • Network/Internet — used only for online multiplayer.
  • Vibration — used for subtle haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • Share — used only when you tap to invite a friend, to open your device’s share sheet with a room code. We do not access your contacts.

5. Advertising & Analytics

This version of the Game does not contain third-party advertising and does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not use cookies for tracking.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Game is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the Game does not require an account and does not collect personal information, it is designed to be safe for younger players. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited data involved in online play. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. Since the Game does not collect sensitive personal information, the privacy risk to you is minimal.

8. Third-Party Services

The online multiplayer server is hosted on a third-party cloud hosting provider, which processes the connection data described in Section 3 on our behalf solely to deliver the service. We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

9. Your Choices

  • Play entirely offline to keep all data on your device.
  • Use any nickname you like — it does not have to be your real name.
  • Clear the App’s local data or uninstall the App at any time to remove stored information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
