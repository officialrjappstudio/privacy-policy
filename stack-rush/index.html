Introduction

Welcome to Stack Rush, developed by RJ App Studio. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles information when you download and play Stack Rush on your Android device.

By using Stack Rush, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the application.

Our commitment Stack Rush does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. We designed this app to be enjoyable while fully respecting your privacy.
02 —

Information Collection and Use

Stack Rush does not collect any personally identifiable information. We do not ask for, access, or store any of the following:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location or GPS data
  • Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Contacts or address book
  • Payment or financial details

The app may access basic Android system information — such as device type and operating system version — solely to ensure the game runs correctly on your device. This information is used only for technical compatibility and is never used to identify you personally.

03 —

Log Data

In the event of an error or crash within the app, your device may automatically generate log data. This can include information such as your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, device name, operating system version, the time and date of the error, and other technical statistics.

This log data is generated by your device's operating system and, if collected at all, is used purely to diagnose and fix technical issues. It is never linked to your identity and is not shared with third parties beyond what is necessary for app stability.

04 —

Cookies

Stack Rush does not use cookies. Cookies are small data files commonly used by websites and some apps to store preferences or track usage. Our app does not employ this technology in any form.

Note While Stack Rush itself does not use cookies, any third-party services we integrate (such as Google Play Services) may use their own identifiers. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details.

Third-Party Services

Stack Rush may integrate with third-party services that operate under their own privacy policies. These services are independent of RJ App Studio, and we encourage you to review their policies to understand how they handle data.

  • Google Play Services — Manages app distribution, updates, and may collect anonymized usage statistics as per Google's privacy policy.

We do not control how these third-party services collect or use data and are not responsible for their privacy practices. We only integrate services that are widely trusted and whose use is necessary for the app to function on the Android platform.

06 —

Data Security

Because Stack Rush does not collect personal data, there is no personal information stored on our servers to protect. We believe the most secure data is data that is never collected in the first place.

We do follow industry-standard practices in the development of our app to ensure it behaves safely and responsibly on your device. However, please be aware that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of any data processed by your device or third-party services.

07 —

Children's Privacy

Stack Rush is designed to be enjoyed by players of all ages. We are committed to protecting the privacy of children.

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect personal data at all, Stack Rush is safe for younger players in this regard.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at the email address below. We will take prompt steps to address the concern.
