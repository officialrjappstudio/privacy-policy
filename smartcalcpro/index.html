Information We Collect

We collect absolutely nothing.

SmartCalc PRO does not collect, request, store, process, or transmit any personal data, sensitive data, or any other information from its users. This includes but is not limited to:

  • ✗ Name, email address, phone number, or any contact information

  • ✗ Usage data, calculation history, or in-app behavior
  • ✗ Financial information, payment data, or billing details
  • ✗ Photos, files, microphone, or camera data
  • ✗ Contacts, calendar, or any other on-device data
02

How the App Works

SmartCalc PRO is a fully offline, on-device application. Every one of the 16 calculators — from the Scientific Calculator to the Loan EMI and Currency Converter — processes all inputs and outputs locally on your smartphone or tablet.

💡 No data ever leaves your device. There are no background sync processes, no cloud backups of your calculations, and no communication with any external server initiated by the app.

Calculation results, inputs, and history (if stored) remain exclusively on your device's local storage and are never transmitted anywhere.

Since we collect no data, there is nothing to share. SmartCalc PRO does not share any information with:

No Analytics or Tracking

SmartCalc PRO does not integrate any analytics, crash reporting, or behavioral tracking SDKs. We do not use any of the following or similar services:

  • ✗ Google Analytics / Firebase Analytics
  • ✗ Facebook SDK or Meta Pixel
  • ✗ Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar tools
  • ✗ Crashlytics or Sentry crash tracking
  • ✗ Any session recording or heatmap software
🔒 Your in-app behavior, screen interactions, and calculator usage are never monitored or recorded.
05

No Advertising

SmartCalc PRO contains no advertisements of any kind. We do not use any advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, or any other ad network) within the app. Therefore, no advertising identifiers or user profiles are created or shared.

You will never see banner ads, interstitial ads, rewarded ads, or any other form of advertising within SmartCalc PRO.

06

Internet Permission

SmartCalc PRO may request internet access solely for the purpose of fetching live currency exchange rates for the Currency Converter feature. This is the only network request the app makes.

Important: When fetching exchange rates, only a standard API call is made to a public rates provider. No user data, device identifiers, or personal information is attached to or transmitted with this request. The request is purely for retrieving publicly available financial data.

All other 15 calculators function entirely offline with zero internet connectivity required.

Data Security

The best data security is no data collection at all. Since SmartCalc PRO does not collect or store any user data on external servers, there is no risk of a data breach, unauthorized access, or data leak affecting our users.

🔐 Any preferences or history stored by the app exist only on your local device and are protected by your device's own operating system security (Android's sandboxing model).

You can delete all local app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data via your device's Settings menu.
