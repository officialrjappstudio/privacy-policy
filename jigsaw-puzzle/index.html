This app provides a jigsaw puzzle experience for users. It does not require account creation or sign-in.

The project may contain an optional Google Play Billing product for removing ads or unlocking app features. If a purchase is made, payment processing is handled by Google Play. The app does not store payment card details itself.

The app stores a small amount of data locally on the device, such as sound settings, rate-us popup state, and privacy-policy acknowledgement. These settings stay on the device and are used only to remember the user's preferences.

External links

The app may include links to external pages such as this privacy policy, Google Play pages, or other RJ App Studio pages. If you open a third-party link, you will be directed to that site, and its privacy policy will apply.

What the app does not do

The app does not require account registration, does not directly collect personal identity information for account creation, and does not store payment card details.

Children's privacy

If the app is used by children, parents or guardians should supervise app usage and review any external links or purchases where applicable.
