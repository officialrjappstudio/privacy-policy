Chess Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 15, 2025  ·  Last updated: November 23, 2026

🛡️
No account, no personal details.

Chess needs no sign-up and never asks for your name, email or phone number. Ads are rewarded videos only, shown only when you choose to watch one.

📋 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the Chess app ("App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

You can play against the computer, with a friend on one phone, and the daily puzzle without an internet connection and without any account. Online play and rewarded videos are optional and need a connection.

🚫 Data We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any of the following:

  • Personal information (real name, email, phone number, address)
  • Precise location (GPS)
  • Contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files
  • Usage analytics or gameplay statistics on our own servers

💾 Local Device Storage

The App saves the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your settings (sound, music, language, reduced motion)
  • Your unfinished game, so you can carry on later
  • Daily puzzle progress and streaks
  • How many free hints and take-backs you have used today

This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

🎬 Advertising

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show rewarded video ads only — never banner, pop-up or full-screen ads between moves. A video plays only when you choose to watch one, by pressing a clearly labelled “Watch video” button, in return for a reward in the game (for example extra hints). You can always say “No thanks”.

To deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure results, Google and its partners may process device and network information, such as the advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from that IP address, and general device characteristics. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings › Ad choices in the App.

You can also limit ad personalisation or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings (on Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads).

🌐 Online Play & Internet Access

The App uses the internet only for rewarded videos, for optional online games and for anonymous usage statistics (see Third-Party Services). If you play online, the display name you type, your moves and your connection details (such as IP address) are sent to our game server so that two players can be matched and play. They are used only to run the game and are not sold or used for advertising. No account is created.

The App does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

🔗 Third-Party Services

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform — rewarded video ads and the consent message, as described above.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage statistics (for example: a game was started or finished, a puzzle was solved, an ad offer was shown), together with an app-instance identifier, the advertising identifier and general device information. We use it only to see which parts of the App are used and to improve them. Where consent is required, nothing is sent until you consent. See the Google Privacy Policy.

The App does not use social login and does not include crash-reporting SDKs.

👶 Children's Privacy

Chess is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

🔒 Data Security

Game data saved on your device is protected by Android's standard app sandboxing — no other app can access it.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and post the new policy here.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
