Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

123 Numbers (com.rjappstudio.onetwothreenumberskidsfunworld) is published by RJ App Studio.

What the app does

This app is an educational game for learning numbers. It does not require account creation or sign-in.

Advertising

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner and interstitial ads. Google and its partners may process device and network information that is needed to deliver ads, prevent fraud, measure ad performance, and maintain the ads service.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

In-app purchases

The project contains an optional Google Play Billing product named iap_no_ads. If a purchase is made, payment processing is handled by Google Play. The app does not store payment card details itself.

On-device preferences

The app stores a small amount of data locally on the device using Unity PlayerPrefs, including music settings, rate-us popup state, and privacy-policy acknowledgement. These settings stay on the device and are used only to remember the player's preferences.

External links

The app can open this privacy policy URL and Google Play listing pages for this app and other RJ App Studio apps.

What the app does not do

  • No account registration
  • No user-generated content upload
  • No Firebase Analytics or custom analytics SDK after the March 14, 2026 compliance update

Children's privacy

This app is intended as a child-focused educational experience. Parents or guardians should review this policy and supervise external links or purchases.
